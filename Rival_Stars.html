What Is Rival Stars?

Rival Stars is a sports gaming franchise known for competitive gameplay and immersive experiences. Depending on the edition, players can explore horse racing, team management, breeding, training, and other sports-related activities.

The games combine progression, competition, and customization to create an experience for players who enjoy sports-themed adventures. Its different editions offer different gameplay styles, giving fans several ways to explore the Rival Stars universe.
