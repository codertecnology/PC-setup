Offline Text-to-Speech Software

Text to Speech Studio – Offline by Illusanato

Turn written words into spoken audio with a desktop text-to-speech application. Text to Speech Studio – Offline is designed to generate synthetic speech directly on your Windows PC, without requiring an internet connection or an online account.

What Is Text to Speech Studio – Offline?

Text to Speech Studio – Offline is a Windows application developed by Illusanato. It converts written text into spoken audio using built-in synthetic voices.

The application is designed for users who want to create narration, listen to written material, prepare voice content, or generate audio files without uploading their text to an online service.

Unlike browser-based text-to-speech platforms, this software performs its speech generation locally on the computer. It includes a collection of voices and supports exporting generated audio as MP3 files with subtitles.

Key Features of the Software

Offline Speech Generation

Convert written content into spoken audio directly on your computer without depending on an internet connection.

Multiple AI Voices

Choose from a collection of 59 voices across nine languages, including included studio voices and supported Windows voices.

MP3 Export

Export generated speech as MP3 audio files, with subtitle support for suitable projects.

Multiple Character Voices

Assign different voices to individual characters when preparing dialogue or multi-character narration.

Voice Customization

Explore available voice settings and adjust speech characteristics to suit your listening or narration needs.

Voice Library

Browse available voices by name or language, filter options, save favorites, and preview voices before using them.

Local Processing

Speech generation runs on your computer's processor. A dedicated graphics card is not required.

No Account Required

The application does not require an account or subscription to use its included desktop features.

Supported Languages and Voices

One of the useful features of Text to Speech Studio – Offline is its collection of voices for different languages. The application advertises 59 voices across nine languages.

Its included voice collection covers English (US and UK), Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Hindi, Japanese, and Mandarin Chinese. It also lists compatible speech voices already installed in Windows.

Users can browse the voice library, search by language or voice name, filter available options, and listen to previews before selecting a voice.

Why Choose an Offline Text-to-Speech Tool?

Online speech generators can be useful, but they often require an internet connection and may involve uploading written content. An offline application provides a different workflow.

  • Work without internet: Generate speech even when your computer is disconnected.
  • Keep text on your PC: Process written content locally rather than sending it to an online conversion service.
  • Create narration: Prepare audio for presentations, personal projects, and educational material.
  • Listen to written content: Convert articles, notes, and other text into spoken audio.
  • Choose different voices: Compare available voices and select suitable options for different types of content.
Privacy information: The developer describes the application as offline-only, with no account, telemetry, or network features. Generated speech is synthetic and does not clone a real person's voice.

How to Use Text to Speech Studio – Offline

Getting started with the application is straightforward. Follow these general steps to generate your first audio file.

1

Install the software: Download the application from its official distribution source and follow the Windows installation instructions.

2

Open the application: Launch Text to Speech Studio – Offline on your computer.

3

Enter your text: Type or paste the written content that you want to convert into spoken audio.

4

Select a voice: Browse the available voices and choose one that matches your preferred language and narration style.

5

Preview the speech: Listen to the selected voice and review how the text sounds before exporting.

6

Export your audio: Generate the speech and save the result as an MP3 file with subtitles where supported.

System Requirements

Text to Speech Studio – Offline is designed for Windows desktop computers. Check the following requirements before installing the application.

  • Operating System: Windows 10 version 1809 or newer.
  • Architecture: 64-bit Windows.
  • Storage: Approximately 1.2 GB of disk space.
  • Graphics Card: No dedicated GPU required.
  • Internet: Not required for application use after installation.
Note: Make sure your PC has sufficient free storage for installation and voice resources.

Pricing and Trial Information
