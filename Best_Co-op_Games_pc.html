Best Co-op Games for PC

Team up with your friends, complete exciting missions, survive dangerous worlds, and enjoy unforgettable multiplayer adventures on PC.

Play Together and Have More Fun

Co-op games let players work together instead of competing against each other. You can explore massive worlds, solve puzzles, fight powerful enemies, or build amazing creations with your friends.

Whether you have a gaming squad of four players or just one friend to play with, PC offers plenty of fantastic cooperative experiences.

GAME 01

It Takes Two

Enjoy a creative two-player adventure filled with puzzles, platforming, teamwork, and memorable challenges. The entire experience is designed around cooperation.

GAME 02

Split Fiction

Travel through imaginative science-fiction and fantasy worlds in this exciting two-player adventure that constantly introduces new gameplay ideas.

GAME 03

Deep Rock Galactic

Join a team of space miners, explore dangerous caves, collect valuable resources, and fight alien creatures in cooperative missions.

GAME 04

Phasmophobia

Investigate haunted locations with your friends, collect paranormal evidence, and try to identify mysterious ghosts.

GAME 05

Portal 2

Solve clever physics-based puzzles with a partner using portals, teamwork, and creative problem-solving.

GAME 06

Valheim

Explore a vast Viking-inspired world, build settlements, craft equipment, and defeat powerful creatures alongside your friends.

GAME 07

Lethal Company

Collect scrap from abandoned industrial locations while avoiding dangerous creatures. Communication and teamwork are essential for survival.

GAME 08

Stardew Valley

Build a farm, grow crops, raise animals, explore mines, and enjoy a relaxing cooperative farming adventure.

Which Co-op Game Should You Choose?

For two players, try It Takes Two or Split Fiction. If you enjoy horror, Phasmophobia and Lethal Company are exciting choices. For long-term world-building, explore Valheim or Stardew Valley.

Online Co-op or Local Co-op?

Online co-op lets you play with friends over the internet, even when everyone is using a different computer. Local co-op allows players to share one PC and often one screen.

Before buying a game, check its multiplayer features, supported player count, and system requirements. Some games support both online and local co-op, while others offer only one.
