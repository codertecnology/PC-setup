What Is TextSniper?

TextSniper is a macOS application designed to extract text from areas of your screen. It uses Optical Character Recognition (OCR) technology to recognize written content in images, videos, presentations, and other visual material.

Instead of manually typing words from a screenshot or document, users can select a portion of their screen and let TextSniper recognize the text. This can make everyday computer tasks faster and more convenient.

Quick Overview: TextSniper is especially useful when you need to copy text that normally cannot be selected, such as words appearing inside an image, video, or scanned document.

How Does TextSniper Work?

TextSniper is designed to make text extraction simple. The general workflow involves selecting an area, recognizing its text, and copying the result.

Open the Application

Launch TextSniper on your Mac.

Select the Screen Area

Choose the part of the screen containing the text.

Recognize the Text

The OCR feature processes the selected content.

Copy and Use

Copy the recognized text and paste it wherever needed.
