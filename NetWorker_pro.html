What Is NetWorker Pro for Mac?

NetWorker Pro is a lightweight network monitoring application designed for Mac users who want to understand their internet connection more easily. It displays useful network information directly in the macOS menu bar, making important statistics accessible while you work.

Instead of repeatedly opening network settings or other monitoring applications, users can check current upload and download speeds from a convenient menu-bar interface. It can also show traffic information for a selected network adapter.

Why Use NetWorker Pro on macOS?

Internet speed can change depending on network conditions, connected devices, downloads, and other online activities. NetWorker Pro provides a convenient way to observe network activity while using your computer.

Its menu-bar approach keeps monitoring information accessible without requiring a large application window. This can be especially useful for people who regularly download files, attend online meetings, upload content, or work with cloud-based services.

Quick Tip: Use the speed display to observe your connection during downloads and uploads. Remember that the displayed speed is a measurement of network activity, not a guarantee of your internet provider's advertised speed.
