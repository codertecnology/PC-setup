What Is Mac FoneLab for iOS?

Mac FoneLab for iOS is a data recovery application designed to help users retrieve lost or deleted information from iPhones, iPads, and supported iOS backups. Three Available Recovery Methods

One of the useful aspects of FoneLab is its multiple recovery options. The appropriate method depends on where your missing information is stored.

1. Recover from an iOS Device

Connect your iPhone or iPad to your Mac and use the device recovery mode to scan supported information. After scanning, review the results and select the files you want to export.

2. Recover from an iTunes Backup

If you previously synchronized your iPhone with a computer, an available backup may contain information that is no longer present on your device. FoneLab can scan supported backup files and help retrieve selected data.

3. Recover from an iCloud Backup

This option allows users to work with supported iCloud backup information. After accessing and scanning the available backup, users can preview and select files for recovery.

Important: Data recovery is not guaranteed. Results depend on the device, backup availability, file condition, and whether deleted data has been overwritten. Always keep a separate backup of important information.
