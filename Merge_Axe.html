What Is Merge Axe?

Merge Axe is a casual idle merging game developed by PuLu Network. It takes players into a creative blacksmithing world where they can collect different axes, combine identical items, and unlock more powerful variations. The game offers an enjoyable combination of merging, crafting, and resource management.

Players begin as beginner blacksmiths with a small shop. By purchasing axes and merging matching items, they can discover new designs and gradually develop their blacksmithing business. The game features more than 50 unique axe varieties to discover.

Merge Axe Gameplay

The main gameplay revolves around combining identical axes to create higher-level versions. Each successful merge helps players unlock different axe designs and improve their collection.

Higher-level axes can generate more valuable coins, allowing players to expand their shop and continue progressing. The game also introduces resource management and trading elements, including interactions with goblins.

Its idle gameplay style makes Merge Axe suitable for players who enjoy casual games with simple mechanics and gradual progression.
