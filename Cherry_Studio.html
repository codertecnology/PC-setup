What Is Cherry Studio?

Artificial intelligence is changing the way people communicate, create content, research information, and complete everyday computer tasks. However, working with multiple AI platforms can become inconvenient when users need to switch between different websites and applications.

Cherry Studio is an AI desktop client designed to bring different artificial intelligence models and productivity features together in one interface. It allows users to connect supported AI providers, create customized assistants, manage conversations, and work with documents.

The application also includes tools for AI image generation, translation, knowledge management, and agent-based workflows. It is available for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Connect Different AI Providers

One of Cherry Studio's important capabilities is its support for multiple AI providers. Users can configure compatible services and select different models depending on their requirements.

Supported integrations include providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, and DeepSeek. Local model connections through supported tools such as Ollama and LM Studio are also available.

AI Models and Integrations

Cherry Studio makes it easier to organize different model connections. Rather than maintaining separate desktop interfaces for every compatible provider, users can manage supported integrations within the application.

  • Supports local knowledge bases and document-based questions.
