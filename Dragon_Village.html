Dragon Village – Discover a Magical Dragon World

Collect legendary dragons, hatch mysterious eggs, train powerful creatures, and become a master Dragon Tamer.

What Is Dragon Village?

Dragon Village is a fantasy-themed dragon collection and adventure game developed by Highbrow. It invites players to become Dragon Tamers and explore a magical world filled with legendary creatures. The main objective is to discover dragon eggs, hatch different species, and train dragons to become stronger.

The game features hundreds of distinct dragons with different appearances, abilities, and elemental powers. Players can explore various environments, collect rare creatures, and develop their dragon collection through multiple evolution stages.

Dragon Village Gameplay

The gameplay begins with discovering mysterious dragon eggs hidden throughout different locations. Players can hatch these eggs and raise their dragons by developing their abilities and improving their strength.

Each dragon has its own characteristics and elemental identity. The game includes fire, earth, water, wind, light, and dark elements. Training and evolving dragons helps players prepare for different challenges and powerful opponents.

Dragon Village also includes competitive features where players can challenge other Dragon Tamers and work toward achieving better leaderboard positions.

Graphics and Fantasy World

Dragon Village features a colorful fantasy environment filled with imaginative dragon designs and different locations. Each dragon has its own visual identity, elemental characteristics, and signature moves.

From mysterious exploration areas to dangerous fantasy environments, the game provides different places to discover new creatures and continue your adventure. Its collection-focused gameplay makes it interesting for fans of fantasy and creature-raising games.

Platforms and Availability

Dragon Village by Highbrow is available on Android through the Google Play Store. The game is free to download and includes advertisements and optional in-app purchases.
