Best MacBook Games

Looking for exciting games to play on your MacBook? Discover a collection of popular Mac games featuring action, adventure, strategy, racing, and relaxing gameplay experiences.

Enjoy Gaming on Your MacBook

MacBook computers are becoming increasingly capable gaming machines. Apple Silicon processors and improved macOS game support have made it possible to enjoy many popular titles directly on a Mac.

Whether you own a MacBook Air or MacBook Pro, you can find games that match your interests. Some games are lightweight and suitable for casual play, while others require more powerful hardware and additional memory.

Tips for Playing Games on MacBook

Make Your Mac Gaming Experience Better

  • Check the game's macOS requirements before downloading.
  • Prefer native Apple Silicon games when available.
  • Keep macOS and your games updated.
  • Lower graphics settings if the game runs slowly.
  • Keep enough free storage for large game files.
  • Use a compatible controller for supported games.
  • For demanding games, check your MacBook's chip and RAM.

Can You Play Steam Games on MacBook?

Yes, many Steam games are available for macOS. However, not every game on Steam supports Mac. Before purchasing a game, check whether its store page lists macOS compatibility.

Some Windows-only games may work through compatibility tools or cloud gaming services, but results can vary. Native Mac versions are usually the simplest option.
