What Is jAlbum?

Photographs are an important way to preserve memories, showcase creative projects, and share experiences with other people. However, managing hundreds or thousands of images can become difficult without the right tools. jAlbum is a desktop photo gallery software application that helps users transform their image collections into attractive online photo albums.

Instead of manually designing every gallery page, users can import their pictures, organize them into folders, select a visual theme, and generate a complete photo gallery. The software also supports video galleries and provides options for customizing the presentation of digital content.

jAlbum is available for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Users can publish their finished galleries to their own websites or use the jAlbum hosting service.

How Does jAlbum Work?

One of the useful aspects of jAlbum is its straightforward gallery creation workflow. You do not need to manually write HTML code to generate a basic photo album. The application handles the gallery generation process.

Step 01 - Import Your Photos

Start by adding photographs and supported video files from your computer. You can drag and drop files directly into your project.

Step 02 - Organize Your Collection

Create folders, arrange images, rotate photographs, and add captions to make your album easier to browse.

Step 03 - Choose a Gallery Design

Select a skin that matches your preferred presentation style. Customize its appearance and available settings.

Step 04 - Generate Your Album

Use the Make Album option to generate your photo gallery and preview its appearance before publishing.

Step 05 - Publish and Share

Upload your finished gallery to your own website through FTP or publish it using the available jAlbum hosting options.
