WALLPAPER COLLECTION 2026

Best Wallpaper Apps

Transform your smartphone and desktop with beautiful HD, 4K, AMOLED, artistic and live wallpapers. Discover popular apps that make personalizing your screen simple and enjoyable.

01. Introduction

Wallpapers are one of the easiest ways to personalize a smartphone, tablet or computer. A beautiful background can give your device a fresh appearance and reflect your interests, personality and creative style.

Today, wallpaper applications offer thousands of designs in different categories, including nature, cars, technology, gaming, abstract art, landscapes, minimalism and photography. Some apps also provide animated backgrounds, automatic wallpaper changes and custom wallpaper creation tools.

Whether you prefer a simple background or a colorful live wallpaper, the following apps offer different ways to customize your screen in 2026.

02. Top Wallpaper Apps

🎨

1. Zedge

Zedge is a popular personalization app offering a large collection of wallpapers, live backgrounds, ringtones and notification sounds. Users can explore different categories and discover designs for various screen styles.

It also offers additional personalization features, including AI-assisted wallpaper creation in supported versions. Some content and premium features require payment.

Best for variety and personalization
🖼️

2. Backdrops

Backdrops focuses on original artwork and carefully selected wallpaper designs. Its collection includes minimalist patterns, creative illustrations and backgrounds designed for modern smartphone displays.

The Wall of the Day feature helps users discover fresh artwork. Some collections and additional features are available through premium options.

Best for original and minimalist designs
🌈

3. Walli

Walli is designed for people who enjoy creative, artist-made wallpapers. It offers illustrations, digital artwork and photography from different creators.

Users can explore artist profiles and discover different artistic styles. It is a good choice for anyone who wants something more creative than ordinary background images.

Best for artist-made wallpapers
📱

4. Wallcraft

Wallcraft provides wallpaper collections designed for smartphone screens. It offers different visual styles, including 3D-inspired images, colorful artwork, nature scenes and live wallpaper options.

Its device-oriented collections can help users find backgrounds that fit their display. Availability of specific effects depends on the device and app version.

Best for 3D and live-style backgrounds
📷

5. Unsplash

Unsplash is a photography platform with a large collection of high-quality images. Users can discover beautiful landscapes, architecture, animals, cities and nature photography for their screens.

It is particularly useful for people who prefer realistic photographs instead of illustrated designs. Its images can also provide inspiration for creative projects.

Best for high-quality photography
🔷

6. Tapet

Tapet offers a different approach to wallpaper personalization by generating unique patterns and abstract backgrounds. Instead of browsing only downloaded images, users can create new designs directly within the application.

It also provides automatic wallpaper-changing functionality, making it useful for people who like refreshing their home screen regularly.

Best for automatically generated patterns
🎭

7. Vellum Wallpapers

Vellum Wallpapers is an iPhone-focused application featuring curated background collections. Its daily wallpaper selection makes it easy to discover a new visual style without searching through thousands of images.

The app also includes image customization features such as blur effects. It is a useful option for people who prefer carefully selected designs.

Best for curated iPhone wallpapers
🌀

8. Muzei

Muzei is an Android wallpaper application that can automatically refresh your background with artwork from supported sources. It offers a convenient way to enjoy changing images without manually selecting a new wallpaper every time.

Users can explore art-focused backgrounds and customize how images appear on their screens. Its open-source nature also makes it interesting for users who appreciate flexible software.

Best for automatically changing artwork
✨

9. Abstruct

Abstruct focuses on colorful abstract wallpaper collections with distinctive artistic styles. It is suitable for users who enjoy modern shapes, gradients and visually creative backgrounds.

The app offers high-resolution artwork and collections that can give a smartphone a distinctive appearance. Some content may require a premium upgrade.

Best for abstract and high-resolution art

03. How to Choose the Right Wallpaper App

Things to Consider Before Installing

  • Image quality: Look for HD, 4K or device-resolution images to keep backgrounds sharp.
  • Design variety: Choose an app with categories that match your personal taste.
  • Live wallpapers: Check whether the app supports animated backgrounds on your device.
  • AMOLED support: Dark backgrounds with true black areas can look especially good on OLED screens.
  • Automatic changes: Consider apps that refresh wallpapers on a daily or scheduled basis.
  • Ads and subscriptions: Review free and paid features before purchasing a premium plan.
  • Battery usage: Animated backgrounds may use more battery than static images.

04. Benefits of Wallpaper Apps

Wallpaper apps make it easier to customize devices without searching manually across different websites. They bring together collections of photographs, illustrations, patterns and animated backgrounds.

Users can change their device appearance whenever they want, discover new creative styles and create a more personalized digital experience. Some apps also provide tools for generating custom backgrounds and automatically rotating favorite images.

Final Thoughts

The best wallpaper app depends on your device and personal preferences. Zedge is a strong choice for variety, Backdrops and Walli offer creative artwork, Unsplash is excellent for photography, and Tapet provides automatically generated patterns.

iPhone users may also enjoy Vellum, while Android users can explore Muzei for automatically changing artwork. Try different apps, compare their image quality and choose the one that makes your screen look exactly how you want.
