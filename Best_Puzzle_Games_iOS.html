Best Puzzle Games for iOS

Challenge your mind, solve clever mysteries, and enjoy relaxing puzzle adventures on your iPhone or iPad.

Explore Amazing iOS Puzzle Games

Puzzle games are a fantastic way to enjoy mobile entertainment while testing your logic, creativity, and problem-solving skills. The App Store offers everything from simple matching games to mind-bending adventures.

Whether you want a quick game during a break or a challenging experience that keeps you thinking, these iOS puzzle games are worth exploring.

PUZZLE 01

Monument Valley

Explore beautiful optical illusions, manipulate impossible architecture, and guide a character through imaginative geometric worlds.

PUZZLE 02

Gorogoa

Solve creative visual puzzles by arranging and combining illustrated panels. Its artistic presentation makes every puzzle feel unique.

PUZZLE 03

The Room: Old Sins

Investigate mysterious objects, discover hidden mechanisms, and solve detailed mechanical puzzles in an atmospheric adventure.

PUZZLE 04

Brain Test: Tricky Puzzles

Enjoy funny brain teasers that require creative thinking, unexpected answers, and a fresh approach to solving problems.

PUZZLE 05

Block Blast!

Place blocks on a grid, complete rows and columns, and try to achieve a high score in this addictive puzzle game.

PUZZLE 06

Candy Crush Saga

Match colorful candies, complete challenging levels, and enjoy a classic match-three experience.

PUZZLE 07

Flow Free

Connect matching colors with pipes and fill the board without crossing paths. Simple rules create clever challenges.

PUZZLE 08

Baba Is You

Change the rules of each level by moving word blocks. This inventive puzzle game rewards experimentation and logical thinking.

Which Puzzle Game Should You Choose?

For beautiful visuals, try Monument Valley. If you enjoy tricky logic, explore Baba Is You. For relaxing gameplay, Flow Free is a great option, while The Room: Old Sins is ideal for mystery and object-based puzzles.

Why Puzzle Games Are Great on iOS

Puzzle games often use simple touch controls, making them a natural fit for iPhone and iPad. Many can be played in short sessions, while deeper puzzle adventures offer hours of discovery.

Some titles are free to download, while others require a purchase or offer optional in-app purchases. Check the App Store listing for current pricing and device compatibility.
