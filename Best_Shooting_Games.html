Best Shooting Games for Android

Get ready for intense battles, competitive multiplayer, tactical missions, and exciting shooting adventures on your Android smartphone.

Discover the Best Android Shooters

Shooting games are among the most exciting genres available on Android. From realistic battlefield experiences to fast-paced battle royale matches, mobile shooters offer something for every gaming fan.

Whether you prefer playing with friends, competing against online players, or completing action-packed missions, these games are worth exploring.

GAME 01

Call of Duty: Mobile

Enjoy classic multiplayer battles, battle royale, customizable weapons, and exciting seasonal content. It is a great choice for players who want different shooting modes in one game.

GAME 02

PUBG MOBILE

Enter large battle royale matches, collect weapons, work with your squad, and try to become the last team standing.

GAME 03

Free Fire MAX

Experience quick battle royale matches, character abilities, and intense survival gameplay designed for mobile players.

GAME 04

Blood Strike

Jump into fast-paced shooting action with battle royale and other competitive modes. It is suitable for players who enjoy quick combat.

GAME 05

Standoff 2

Test your aim and teamwork in competitive first-person shooter matches. Its tactical gameplay rewards accuracy and smart movement.

GAME 06

Critical Ops

Take part in tactical team battles where communication, quick reactions, and precise shooting can make the difference.

GAME 07

Arena Breakout

Enter high-risk missions, search for valuable equipment, and try to escape with your loot in this tactical extraction shooter.

GAME 08

Delta Force

Experience modern military action with large-scale battles, tactical missions, and team-based combat.

Best Shooting Game for Your Play Style

Choose Call of Duty: Mobile for variety, PUBG MOBILE for battle royale, Standoff 2 for competitive FPS matches, or Arena Breakout if you enjoy tactical extraction gameplay.

Tips for a Better Android Shooting Experience

Adjust your sensitivity settings to improve aiming. Use headphones to hear important sounds during matches, and close unnecessary background apps before playing.

Multiplayer shooting games usually require an internet connection. Check the Google Play listing for your country to confirm availability, device compatibility, storage requirements, and age rating before installing.
