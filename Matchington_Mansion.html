Matchington Mansion

Match colorful puzzles, renovate a beautiful mansion, discover hidden secrets, and create stunning interior designs in this relaxing home makeover adventure.

What Is Matchington Mansion?

Matchington Mansion is an entertaining match-3 puzzle and home renovation game developed by Magic Tavern, Inc. It combines colorful matching challenges with creative interior decoration and an engaging story. Players solve puzzles to earn rewards and transform an old mansion into a beautiful and stylish home.

The game offers an enjoyable combination of puzzle solving, room customization, and character interactions. As you progress through different levels, you unlock new areas, discover interesting objects, and explore the secrets surrounding the mansion.

Enjoy Fun Match-3 Puzzle Gameplay

The main gameplay revolves around matching colorful pillows or other puzzle pieces to complete level objectives. Players swap pieces, create matching combinations, and use special boosters to overcome challenging stages.

Each completed level helps you earn stars and rewards that can be used to renovate different parts of your mansion. The combination of strategic matching and home improvement makes every completed puzzle feel rewarding.

Decorate Rooms and Design Your Dream Mansion

One of the most enjoyable parts of Matchington Mansion is its home decoration system. Players can renovate different rooms by selecting furniture, colors, decorations, and other design elements.

You can gradually improve the mansion and unlock additional areas as you progress. From comfortable living spaces to beautiful gardens, the game offers plenty of creative opportunities for players who enjoy interior design.

Meet Characters and Discover Hidden Secrets

Matchington Mansion is not only about completing puzzles and decorating rooms. It also features an adventure story with interesting characters, unexpected events, and hidden secrets.

Tiffany and other memorable characters help make the experience more engaging. As the story develops, players discover new details about the mansion and the people connected to it.
