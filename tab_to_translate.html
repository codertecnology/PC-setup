What Is Tap To Translate Screen 1.126?

Tap To Translate Screen is an Android translation application that helps users understand text displayed in different mobile applications. Its main purpose is to make screen translation more convenient by allowing users to translate visible content with a simple interaction.

The application can be useful for reading messages, understanding foreign social media posts, exploring online shopping platforms, and translating text in games and comics. It also offers additional translation options involving voice, camera, and images.
