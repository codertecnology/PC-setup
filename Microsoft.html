About Microsoft

Microsoft is one of the world's leading technology companies, known for developing software, operating systems, cloud platforms, and digital services. Its products are used by individuals, businesses, students, and organizations around the world.

From Windows operating systems to Microsoft 365 productivity applications, the company provides a wide range of solutions for everyday computing, communication, creativity, and business activities.
