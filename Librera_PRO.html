What Is Librera PRO?

Librera PRO is a digital reading application that helps users open, manage, and read different types of documents on Android devices. Instead of installing separate applications for different book formats, users can manage many supported formats through a single reading platform.

The application also includes reading customization options. You can adjust display settings, use different themes, save bookmarks, and access additional tools depending on the document format.

Top Features of Librera PRO

01. Multiple File Formats

Read popular formats such as PDF, EPUB, MOBI, FB2, TXT, DOCX, RTF, HTML, DjVu, CBZ, and CBR.

02. Custom Reading Experience

Adjust fonts, margins, colors, backgrounds, and other display settings to create a comfortable reading environment.

03. Day and Night Modes

Switch between light and dark reading themes according to your surroundings and personal preferences.

04. Bookmarks and Annotations

Save important pages, highlight useful information, and add annotations while reading supported documents.

05. Text-to-Speech

Use the device's text-to-speech engine to listen to supported documents instead of reading every page manually.

06. Document Search

Find specific words and information inside documents with built-in search functionality.

07. Online Book Catalogs

Browse supported OPDS catalogs to discover and access digital books from compatible online sources.

08. Google Drive Sync

Librera PRO offers synchronization options for reading progress and settings across supported devices.

How to Use Librera PRO on Android

  1. Install the Librera PRO application on your Android device.
  2. Open the application and explore its main interface.
  3. Select the folder containing your ebooks and documents.
  4. Allow the required file access permissions.
  5. Wait for the application to scan your selected folders.
  6. Choose a book or document from your library.
  7. Customize the reading settings according to your needs.
  8. Use bookmarks and other reading tools whenever required.
