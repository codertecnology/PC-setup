What Are World War II Strategy Games?

World War II strategy games are a popular category of historical video games inspired by the events and military environments of the Second World War. These games allow players to experience different historical settings through missions, resource management, tactical decisions, and campaign-based challenges.


Depending on the game, players may manage military units, develop resources, complete objectives, explore historical locations, or make strategic decisions. Some titles focus on real-time strategy, while others use turn-based gameplay. This variety makes the genre interesting for different types of strategy gaming fans.

What Makes This Genre Interesting?

World War II strategy games combine historical inspiration with gameplay that rewards planning, patience, and thoughtful decision-making. Each game offers its own approach to missions, challenges, and progression.

Strategic Thinking

Players often need to consider available resources, objectives, and different possible outcomes.

Immersive Gameplay

Historical maps, environments, sound effects, and campaign stories can create an engaging experience.

Different Challenges

Missions and objectives can change throughout the game, keeping the experience varied.

Learning About History

Some games introduce players to historical events, although game scenarios may simplify or fictionalize history.
