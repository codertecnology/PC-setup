What Is Yate Software?

Yate is known as an audio tag editor for macOS. It allows users to edit and organize metadata associated with music files. Metadata can include artist names, album titles, track numbers, artwork and other useful information.

Properly organized audio metadata makes it easier to browse music libraries, identify songs and maintain a consistent music collection.
