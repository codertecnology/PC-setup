Why Play Mac Games with a Controller?

Playing games with a controller can make gaming more comfortable and enjoyable. Gamepads are especially useful for racing games, platformers, sports titles, and action adventures. Instead of relying entirely on a keyboard and mouse, players can enjoy familiar console-style controls on their Mac.

Quick tip: Controller compatibility depends on the individual game and macOS version. Always check the game's requirements before downloading or purchasing it.

Which Controllers Work with Mac?

Many popular controllers can connect to compatible Macs. Depending on your device and macOS version, you can use Bluetooth or a USB connection.

  • PlayStation DualSense Wireless Controller
  • PlayStation DualShock 4 Controller
  • Xbox Wireless Controller
  • Compatible third-party Bluetooth gamepads
  • Supported wired USB controllers

How to Connect a Controller to Mac

  • Turn on your Bluetooth controller.
  • Activate its Bluetooth pairing mode.
  • Open System Settings on your Mac.
  • Select Bluetooth and find your controller.
  • Click Connect when the controller appears.
  • Open a compatible game and test the controls.

Tips for a Better Controller Gaming Experience

  • Keep your controller charged before playing.
  • Check controller settings inside each game.
  • Use a wired connection if Bluetooth is unstable.
  • Update your macOS version when appropriate.
  • Check Steam's controller compatibility information.
