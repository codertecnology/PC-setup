What Is Space Menace?

Space Menace is a science-fiction real-time strategy (RTS) and space battle game developed and published by Only4Gamers. It puts players in the role of a space captain who must build a fleet, complete missions, and explore a dangerous galaxy.

Starting with a single spaceship, players can gradually expand their fleet, earn resources, and make important decisions that influence their journey. The game combines tactical combat, resource management, and spaceship customization in a top-down 2D environment.

Space Menace Gameplay

Gameplay in Space Menace focuses on controlling multiple spaceships through real-time strategy controls. Instead of directly piloting one ship, players manage their fleet, plan attacks, and decide how to approach different battles.

Players can earn money by accepting freelance missions or fighting enemy ships and collecting valuable salvage. As the fleet grows, new equipment and tactical options help players prepare for increasingly challenging encounters.
