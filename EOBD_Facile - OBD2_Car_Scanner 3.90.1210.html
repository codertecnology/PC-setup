EOBD Facile - OBD2 Car Scanner

Turn your Android smartphone into a convenient car diagnostic tool. EOBD Facile helps drivers read vehicle fault codes, check engine information, and monitor important sensor data through a compatible OBD2 adapter.

Version: 3.90.1210 | Category: Auto & Vehicles

What Is EOBD Facile - OBD2 Car Scanner?

EOBD Facile - OBD2 Car Scanner is an Android application designed to help users understand their vehicle's diagnostic information. By connecting a compatible ELM327 Bluetooth or Wi-Fi adapter to the vehicle's OBD2 port, users can access information from the car's electronic control system.

The application can read diagnostic trouble codes, display descriptions of detected problems, and show real-time information such as engine RPM, vehicle speed, coolant temperature, and other available sensor readings.

It can be useful for drivers who want to learn more about their vehicle's condition before discussing a problem with a mechanic.

Key Features of EOBD Facile

01. Read Engine Fault Codes

Scan supported vehicle systems and view diagnostic trouble codes with descriptions to better understand possible issues.

02. Real-Time Engine Data

Monitor available vehicle information, including engine RPM, coolant temperature, speed, and other sensor readings.

03. Bluetooth & Wi-Fi Support

Connect the application with compatible ELM327 OBD2 adapters using supported Bluetooth or Wi-Fi connections.

04. Vehicle Identification

Use the VIN identification feature to retrieve supported vehicle identification information.

05. Diagnostic Reports

Save and share diagnostic information through available reporting features.

06. Sensor Monitoring

View supported sensor values and use graphs or recording tools where available in the selected edition.

How Does the OBD2 Scanner Work?

The application communicates with your car through an OBD2 diagnostic adapter. The adapter connects to the vehicle's diagnostic port and sends supported information to your phone.

Once connected, you can open the application, select your adapter, and start checking available diagnostic information. The results depend on your vehicle, adapter quality, and supported diagnostic protocols.

How to Use EOBD Facile on Android

  1. Install the EOBD Facile application on your Android device.
  2. Locate your vehicle's OBD2 diagnostic port.
  3. Connect a compatible ELM327 Bluetooth or Wi-Fi adapter.
  4. Turn on the vehicle ignition as instructed by the app.
  5. Open EOBD Facile and select the correct connection type.
  6. Run a diagnostic scan and review the available information.
Important: Clearing a diagnostic trouble code does not repair the underlying problem. Always investigate the cause of a warning light, and consult a qualified mechanic when necessary.

Vehicle Compatibility

EOBD Facile supports vehicles that comply with the OBD-II diagnostic standard. General compatibility includes many gasoline vehicles from 2001, diesel vehicles from 2004, and commercial vehicles from 2007.

Actual compatibility can vary by vehicle model, manufacturer, diagnostic protocol, and adapter. Check the official compatibility information before relying on the application for your car.

Why Choose an OBD2 Car Scanner App?

A car diagnostic application can make vehicle information easier to understand. Instead of seeing only a warning light on the dashboard, drivers may be able to view a diagnostic code and learn what system requires attention.

EOBD Facile brings several diagnostic tools together in one mobile application. It is designed for everyday drivers and users interested in monitoring supported vehicle data.

Final Thoughts

EOBD Facile - OBD2 Car Scanner is a useful option for Android users who want to explore their vehicle's diagnostic information using a compatible OBD2 adapter. Its fault-code reading, live-data monitoring, and reporting features can help users better understand their car.

Keep in mind that the application is a diagnostic aid, not a replacement for professional vehicle inspection or repairs.
